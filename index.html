    The Goal

    Finish all 12 chores before the trust meter runs dry. If it hits zero, the wolf comes and nobody believes you.

    The Chores

    • Hold: hold Space or the big button for the full time. Letting go pauses it.
    • Timing: press Space (or tap) when the marker is in the gold zone.
    • Arrows: enter the arrows in order.
    • Mistakes cost trust. Once you start a chore you stay until it's done.

    Keeping Score

    • 100 per chore, plus 50 per second faster than its time.
    • Mistake-free chores build a streak worth extra points.
    • Catch stray lambs for 75 points each.
    • Win to bank 20 points per second of trust left. Hard scores ×1.5.
